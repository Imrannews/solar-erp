📚 Solar Buying Guide

Important checks before buying solar equipment.

🔍 Panel Authenticity

  • Check exact model number, wattage and datasheet.
  • Verify serial and warranty information where available.
  • Compare grade, technology and physical condition.
  • Do not judge quality by price alone.

⚡ Inverter Selection

  • Check continuous output and surge capability.
  • Match MPPT voltage/current limits with panel strings.
  • Confirm grid, backup and battery requirements.
  • Review warranty and after-sales support.

🔋 Lithium Battery

  • Compare nominal and usable capacity.
  • Check voltage, BMS and inverter compatibility.
  • Review DoD, cycle rating and warranty.
  • Use correct protection and professional installation.

☀️ System Design

  • Understand kW versus kWh.
  • Compare daily load with expected solar production.
  • Allow for temperature, dust, wiring and conversion losses.
  • Final design should be checked by a qualified installer.
