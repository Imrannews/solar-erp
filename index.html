📚 Solar Information & Buying Guide

Useful information pages can be expanded as separate pages later.

🔍 Solar Module Authenticity

  • Check exact model number and datasheet.
  • Match serial/label information with manufacturer records where available.
  • Check warranty terms and authorized seller information.
  • Do not rely on price alone to judge authenticity.

⚡ Inverter Selection

  • Compare continuous output and surge capability.
  • Check MPPT voltage/current limits.
  • Match inverter with panel string design.
  • Confirm grid, backup and battery requirements.

🔋 Lithium Battery Guide

  • Compare nominal versus usable capacity.
  • Check battery voltage and inverter compatibility.
  • Review DoD, cycle rating, BMS and warranty.
  • Use proper protection and installation practices.

☀️ Solar System Basics

  • Understand kW versus kWh.
  • Compare daily energy usage with PV production.
  • Consider peak sun hours and system losses.
  • Final system design should be checked by a qualified installer.
